# Welcome to ProBooks Accounting

ProBooks is the accounting and Gift Aid platform for UK churches and small charities. It tracks members, donations, bank transactions and Gift Aid claims in one place — and it does the HMRC paperwork for you.

This guide walks you through the first thirty minutes after signing in.

## Five-minute setup checklist

If you are setting up a brand-new charity, work through these five tasks once and you'll be ready to use the system day-to-day.

1. **Charity details.** Open *Admin → Charity Settings* and fill in your charity name, registered HMRC Gift Aid number, address and treasurer name. These details appear on every PDF, every email and every Gift Aid CSV you export, so it's worth getting them right up front. See [Charity details](/docs/charity-settings).
2. **Connect your bank.** From *Bank → Bank Connection*, link your charity's main current account through Open Banking. Once connected, ProBooks will pull in transactions every six hours so you don't have to type them. See [Bank connection](/docs/bank).
3. **Invite the rest of the team.** Most charities have one or two people doing the books. Use *Admin → Invite User* to send them a sign-up link, and choose a role (User or Admin). See [Inviting users](/docs/users-and-roles).
4. **Add your members.** Members are the people who give donations. You can add them one-by-one from the Members page, or import a list — but the most common pattern is to add a member the first time they donate, from the *Add Donation* flow. See [Managing members](/docs/members).
5. **Record your first donation.** Click *Add Donation* on the dashboard, choose the giver, enter the amount, fund and date, and save. That's it — the system handles the rest, including marking it ready to claim Gift Aid. See [Recording donations](/docs/donations).

## A quick tour

Once you're signed in, the top navigation gives you everything you need:

- **Dashboard** — month-to-date totals, fund balances, recent activity.
- **Members** — every donor on your register.
- **Donations** — the full giving history.
- **Bank** — your live bank balance and connection status.
- **Admin** — settings, exports, Gift Aid claims, billing and team management.

## Glossary

These terms come up everywhere in the software. It pays to know what they mean.

- **Member** — a person on your charity's register. Whether they donate or not, you may want to keep their address and contact details on file. Members have addresses, postcodes and Gift Aid declarations.
- **Donor** — informal term for a member who has donated. There is no separate "donor" record — they're just members with one or more donations.
- **Donation** — a single act of giving from a member, with a date, amount and fund. Donations are what HMRC pays Gift Aid on.
- **Fund** — what a donation is *for*: General, Building, Ladies Fund, Missions, etc. You define your own fund list under *Admin → Add Donation Type*.
- **Transaction** — a line on your bank statement: rent, utility bill, salary, or a deposit of donations. Transactions are tagged with a *transaction type* (which often matches a fund name, e.g. "Ladies Fund") so you can see fund balances on the dashboard.
- **Gift Aid Claim** — a CSV file submitted to HMRC listing eligible donations, allowing your charity to reclaim 25p of tax on every £1. ProBooks builds the file for you.
- **Platform admin** — the team at ProBooks. We can step in to help with billing, technical issues, or to file Gift Aid claims on your behalf if you've paid for that service.
