# Charity details

Your charity's details appear on every donor PDF, every emailed report, and every Gift Aid CSV you submit to HMRC. Take a few minutes to get them right.

## Where to find this page

*Admin → Charity Settings* (the link is in the footer of the **Setup** card on the admin console).

## What each field is for

- **Charity name** — appears at the top of donor statements, in email signatures, and on the Gift Aid CSV. Use the exact legal name registered with HMRC.
- **HMRC Charity reference / Gift Aid number** — the HMRC-issued reference for Gift Aid claims. Without this, the Gift Aid CSV won't be accepted by HMRC. If you don't have one, register at [HMRC's Charities Online](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-revenue-customs).
- **Charity contact email** — the public email for your charity. This is the *from* address on emails to your members.
- **Phone number** — appears on donor statements as a contact route.
- **Website** — appears on donor statements; optional.
- **Address** — your registered address. Required for HMRC submissions.
- **Treasurer name** — appears in donation receipt emails ("acknowledged by the treasurer").
- **Logo** — uploaded once, then placed on every donor PDF. PNG or JPG, ideally about 400 pixels wide. Keep file sizes small (under 200KB) for fast page loads.

## Why this matters for Gift Aid

The Gift Aid CSV that ProBooks generates includes your HMRC charity reference at the top. If that field is blank or wrong, HMRC will reject the file. Always double-check after editing.

## Why this matters for emails

When ProBooks sends a donation receipt or member-update request, the email body says something like *"Dear [member name], your donation to [charity name] has been acknowledged by the treasurer."* Both `[charity name]` and the from-address come from this page.

## Logo tips

- The logo is shown at the top of donor statement PDFs and in the page header.
- Use a PNG with transparent background if your logo isn't a simple block of colour.
- If the logo looks stretched, try uploading a square or wide rectangular version.

## Changing settings later

Editing the charity name, address, or HMRC reference takes effect immediately — the next PDF or email you generate will use the new values. Any PDF that has already been generated and emailed is unchanged.
