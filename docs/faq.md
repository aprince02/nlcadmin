# Frequently asked

## Can I delete a donation?

Yes — open the member's giving history, find the donation, click *Delete*. The exception is donations that are part of a Gift Aid claim already submitted to HMRC; those can't be deleted because it would break the audit trail. Contact support if you need to undo a submitted claim.

## Can I undo a Gift Aid claim?

If you self-downloaded the CSV but haven't yet uploaded it to HMRC, contact support and we can reset the claim — donations go back into the Unclaimed pool.

If you've already filed it with HMRC (whether yourself or via our paid service), it can't be undone in ProBooks. You'd need to file a correction with HMRC.

## Can a donor update their own address?

Yes. From the member's edit page, click *Send Update Request*. They'll receive an email with a single-use link valid for 14 days. They can update title, name, email, phone and address. They can't change Gift Aid status or banking name.

## Why didn't an email send?

Most common reasons:

1. The member is marked *Do not email*.
2. The email address looks like a placeholder (e.g. `admin@mail.com`).
3. The member has no email address.

Check the flash message at the top of the page after the action — it's specific about why.

## Can I run multiple charities under one login?

Not directly. Each user account belongs to one charity. If you genuinely act for multiple charities (e.g. you're treasurer of two), use a separate email address for each one and switch between logins.

The ProBooks team can act-as any charity for support purposes — that's a platform-level role, not something a charity user can have.

## Why is the Ladies Fund balance not the same as my donations marked Ladies Fund?

The dashboard balance is calculated from **bank transactions tagged "Ladies Fund"**, not donations. Donations and transactions are separate concepts: a donation is a record of giving; a transaction is the actual money movement at the bank.

When a donation lands in the bank account, it should show up as a transaction with the type set to "Ladies Fund". If transactions aren't tagged correctly, the balance will be off. See [Fund balances](/docs/fund-balances).

## Can I import historical donations?

Yes — contact support with a CSV. We need: member name (or ID), date, amount, fund, Gift Aid eligibility. We'll import in one batch.

## How do I print donor statements?

Go to Members → click *View Giving* on a row → click *Download PDF*. Print from there.

For multiple donors at once, use *Admin → Reports → Export Donations*, choose the date range and fund, and download. That gives you a single PDF with everyone's donations grouped by member.

## What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data is retained for 90 days after cancellation in case you re-subscribe. After 90 days, we contact you about archiving or deletion. To delete immediately, email support.

## Can I export everything?

Yes — contact support and we'll provide a CSV bundle of all your charity's data (members, donations, transactions, Gift Aid claims). Useful as a backup or if you're moving to another system.

## Why does ProBooks ask me to reconnect my bank?

The UK Open Banking standard requires re-authentication every **90 days**. It's a regulator requirement, not something we can change. We'll email you a few days before each expiry.

## How do you handle GDPR?

We're a UK company, EU-hosted, and follow UK GDPR. Your members' personal data is processed under "legitimate interest" (you, the charity, have a clear need to record their donations and contact details). We never sell or share data. Members can request a copy of, or deletion of, their data at any time — contact support.

## Is there a mobile app?

Not yet. ProBooks works in a mobile browser, and the design is responsive — you can record donations and view dashboards on a phone. A native app is on our roadmap once we have more charities using the system.

## Can I do bank reconciliation in ProBooks?

Partly. We pull in every bank transaction and you can match donations against deposits. We don't have a full double-entry bookkeeping reconciliation flow yet — that's planned but not built. For now, ProBooks is best paired with a simple spreadsheet for the closing-balance reconciliation step.

## How do I get help?

Email `support@probooksaccounting.co.uk`. We aim to reply within one working day.
